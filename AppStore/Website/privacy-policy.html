Tubeist Scorer privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Scorer keeps your matches on your device and shares the live scoreboard with the Tubeist device you choose to pair.

Up to Change AB publishes Tubeist Scorer. The app works without an account. Up to Change AB receives app information only when you choose to share it with support.

Match data on your device

Scorer stores team or player names you enter, team colours, scores, sport settings and overlay preferences locally so you can resume scoring after closing the app. The overlay preview uses assets bundled with the app. Local app data may be included in the device backups you enable.

Bluetooth and the broadcast

With your Bluetooth permission, Scorer looks for nearby Tubeist devices. When you enter the pairing code and connect, Scorer sends the graphics and live match data directly to that device. This includes visible details such as team or player names, scores and match status.

The camera operator can include the graphic in a recording or stream. The recording or streaming service and the camera operator’s settings govern what happens to that video. See the Tubeist privacy policy for more about Tubeist.

Control your data and Bluetooth access

  • Edit team or player names in Scorer and use Reset to reset the score for the selected match. Local match data remains in Scorer until you replace it or delete the app.
  • Use Disconnect in Scorer to end the connection. You can also revoke Scorer’s Bluetooth permission in system settings.
  • Use Remove overlay on the receiving Tubeist device to take the graphic off the live video. Manage copies already recorded or streamed where they were saved or shared.

This website

Images, the theme preview and styles are served from this site. The web server may process ordinary connection details, such as your IP address, browser information and the requested page, to deliver and secure the site. Visiting an external link takes you to the selected service.

Support and privacy requests

If you email us, we use your address, message and attachments to respond. Share only what is needed for your request. For questions about this policy or to request access to or deletion of support correspondence, email support@uptochange.com. Retention obligations may apply to support records.
